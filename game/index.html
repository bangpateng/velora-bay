VELORA BAY

SUN-SOAKED OPEN-WORLD CRIME  •  A GAME OF THE BAY, BY THE BAY

You are Kai Marlowe, a courier with a dead-end job and a fast car. Fixer Mara Solis has ten jobs for you — and the Velora Bay Police Department has a file with your name on it. Steal cars, outrun the law, run the story, and own the city: downtown, the marina, the harbor, the hills.

10 story missions • 5-star wanted system • police chases, helicopter & roadblocks • traffic & pedestrians • safehouse, garage & car collection • taxi fares, sun coins & stunt jumps • 3 radio stations • save & continue.

Follow the gold ◆ marker and the minimap. Crimes raise your wanted stars — break line of sight and hide, or reach the safehouse to cool off. Enter the safehouse to save. Buy cars at Dino's Autos. Grab sun coins (30 hidden) for cash. Good luck, Kai.

An original game. Velora Bay, its people and its story are invented — no real city, person or game is copied. Runs offline, no network. v4.
